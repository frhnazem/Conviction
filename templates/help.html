What this is

Conviction is a decision log for crypto traders. Write what you decided and why. It is not a signal service, not a trading bot, and not a promise of profit.

How to write a raw note

Write like a short message to yourself. Messy is OK — Extract will structure it.

  • long btc above 95k, stop 93500, targeting 102
  • skip sol, nothing clean, staying flat
  • fomo buy eth, no stop, chasing the green candle

Weekly review & habits

Weekly review shows counts from your logs and may suggest one habit rule when a pattern shows up often enough. Some weeks you’ll only see counts and no rule — that is normal. Rules are about your process, not trade signals.

Weekly review compares this period to the last one so you can see a habit shrinking.

Mark win/loss and whether a stop was respected, hit, or ignored so revenge and ignored-stop rules can appear.

One habit at a time

The app suggests one habit rule at a time so advice stays clear and warnings stay useful. You can dismiss a rule anytime. If a new rule fits better, accepting it replaces the previous one. You don’t need to manage or rank rules — the app prioritizes automatically. An optional focus theme can prefer one family when it qualifies.

Soft warning

If you accepted a rule and your new log breaks that rule (for example a Buy with FOMO and no invalidation), you’ll see a short warning. You can Log anyway or Edit. Habit rules never hard-block a log.

Your data

Logs stay in your account. Deleted logs are gone — they are not kept for hidden learning. Feedback on the Feedback page is for product development, not a public feed.

Free tier

10 logs per account on the free tier.
